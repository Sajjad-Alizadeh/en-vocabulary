## Notes for accurate learning

- **Q12:** The original entry describes a blood-drinking creature but does not explicitly list the vocabulary word. *Vampire* is my interpretation.
- **Q31:** *Bored* = uninterested; *tired* = needing rest. They are not synonyms.
- **Q32:** *Embarrassed* and *ashamed* overlap, but *ashamed* usually implies guilt or deeper negative feelings.
- **Q34:** *Worried* and *stressed* overlap, but are not interchangeable in every situation.
- **Q41:** Your file says “take about bad luck.” I’ve treated this as the likely typo *talk about bad luck*.
- **Q50:** Your file says “grap.” I’ve treated this as the likely typo *grab*.
- **Q54:** The standard proverb is **“Neither a borrower nor a lender be.”**
- **Q55:** The standard expansion of **ATM** is **automated teller machine**.
- **Q17:** The example in your file says “sign together”; I used “sing together” as a plausible sentence for testing *as well*.
